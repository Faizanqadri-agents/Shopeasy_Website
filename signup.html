Why Register with BloodConnect?

When emergencies occur, hospitals don't have hours to spare. By creating a voluntary profile, you become part of an on-call network that preserves human lives.

Instant Notifications: Receive urgent alerts only when patients match your exact blood group nearby.
Strict Confidentiality: Your personal contact number is never posted on the public internet.
Free Health Records: Track donation dates, hemoglobin trends, and quarterly eligibility safely.

Quick Eligibility Confirmation

  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You weigh a minimum of 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • No major surgery or tattooing in the last 6 months.
